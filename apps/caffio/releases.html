Caffio Release Notes

Caffio is available on the App Store in Japan.

1.4

2026-11-07 · First App Store release

  • Caffio is now available on the App Store in Japan
  • New Pro plans: ¥300/month or ¥3,000/year, with a 2-week free trial
  • Free includes unlimited Bean Notes and Brewing Notes, plus iCloud sync and CSV export
  • Free users can see each cafe's star rating and its latest review on the Map; Pro unlocks all community reviews, taste match and full analytics
  • Yearly Pro members can choose a display name without numbers
  • Delete your account from Settings at any time
  • Record coffee process in more detail, and keep Bean Note drafts if you're interrupted
  • Smarter suggestions for cafes, roasteries and brewing equipment based on what you use

  • Added community reviews for cafes and bars, with the option to share reviews with friends
  • Added a specialty coffee filter to the Map
  • Added a self-roasted toggle for Cafe/Bar bean notes
  • Improved moderation for shared content

1.0

  • First TestFlight build
