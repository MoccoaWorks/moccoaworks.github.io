Caffio

Caffio is a coffee brewing and tasting journal for people who care about the details. Log your beans, dial in your brews, and discover cafes worth visiting.

Currently in TestFlight beta. Not yet available on the App Store.

Bean Notes

Snap a photo of a coffee bag and let OCR pull out the roaster, origin, and roast details. Add tasting notes and keep a searchable history of every bag you've tried.

Brewing Notes

Track ratio, TDS, and extraction for every brew, with charts to help you dial in the cup you're after.

Map

Find cafes and bars nearby, filter for specialty coffee shops, and read community reviews from other Caffio users.

Sync & Export

Keep your notes in sync across devices with iCloud, and export everything to CSV whenever you like.
